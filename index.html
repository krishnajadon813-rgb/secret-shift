EXPLORER FIELD GUIDE

Grow a home.
Follow the shift.

  • Move: WASD / arrows, or drag the touch joystick. The movement direction stays stable until you release.
  • Look: drag only the top camera strip for a short free look; ⌖ recenters behind you. The joystick cannot rotate the camera.
  • Gather & carry: the pack holds 12 items. Stock displays, craft or deliver supplies to make room. Habitat upgrades add carry and shelf space.
  • Stock: gather glowing supplies, visit any display and press E / tap the action button.
  • Sell: guests buy one stocked item when they reach the display and pay glow coins. Empty displays turn guests away.
  • Make: garden press makes nectar; solar recycler makes light cells for keeper requests or market sales.
  • Automate: hire Nori from the side panel; Nori moves a Sunfruit from your pack to the shelf every 24 seconds.
  • SECRET SHIFT: finish three keeper requests, then choose the waterfall route or lantern-root path.
