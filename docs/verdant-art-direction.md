# VERDANT HAVEN — visual direction

## Reference and scope

The initial island concepts and the latest character/shop reference board guide the world, silhouette and palette. The explorer target is a friendly young adventurer with tousled brown hair, a green field top, cream chest mark, work trousers, gloves, boots, goggles and a small pack. The shop target is a welcoming timber eco-market with a green sign, displays, warm lanterns and plants. The customer board shows different ordinary visitors and shop workers; it does not provide a specific robot-helper model.

Reference images are not copied into the game as textures. An image-generation tool is not available in this session, so the changes use original local geometry. The browser renderer is not a rigged character pipeline and cannot reproduce the supplied polished 3D renders exactly.

## Original playable treatment

- **Living areas:** warm moss, leaf green, honey stone and clear turquoise water; broad paths connect a greenhouse press, a small keeper market, habitat and terraced garden.
- **Mystery:** the sealed lantern-root shrine and waterfall bridge become violet/teal signal landmarks. The player's choice opens the water route or the hidden root route.
- **Explorer:** use face and hair geometry, a green field jacket, cream emblem, goggles, work clothes and compact trail pack to make the reference identity legible from the gameplay camera.
- **Market and guests:** frame the existing stocked displays with an open timber storefront. Procedural visitors have six distinct combinations of body proportions, hair, face tone, outfit and accessories, while retaining the same low-poly rendering style.
- **Character and machinery:** retain procedural WebGL materials, then give the island its own glass-roof habitat, solar recycler, raised beds, collectible Sunfruit, salvage and crystal blooms.
- **Performance:** meshes are generated once per island or upgrade change, animated resource markers are batched each frame, and the existing Canvas fallback stays available. Geometry and tones are local and original; no network art, paid asset, or heavy engine is required.

The supplied images are aspirational art references. The current renderer is a lightweight, hand-built procedural low-poly 3D scene, not painted concept art, a textured/rigged character model, or commercial-grade photorealistic assets. The branding and proprietary design details from My Mini Mart are not used.
